KaiTOR Diplomacy v0.6.8.0
Build profile: Family Cap 20 / Race-aware family acceptance
Bannerlord: 1.3.15.110062
TOR target: 1.3.15

Family rules:
- Shared ceiling: 20 living children / accepted successors.
- Biological pregnancy stops when either parent already has 20 living children.
- Adoption/acceptance stops at 20 living children/successors for the player.
- Dawi virtual + accepted/materialized heir slots share the same 20-slot ceiling.
- TOR child education from v0.6.7 remains enabled.

Race-aware acceptance UI:
- Human / Necromancer: "Принять в семью".
- Elf: "Принять в дом".
- Dawi: "Признать наследником клана".
- Vampire / Necrarch: "Принять в кровный дом".
- Greenskin (Orc/Goblin): "Принять в племя".
- Ordinary undead fallback: "Признать частью дома".

Greenskin note:
- Orc and Goblin can be accepted across the two Greenskin races into the same tribe.
- This is social/tribal succession only. It does not enable biological reproduction.
- The accepted hero becomes part of the player's succession/family graph for Bannerlord compatibility.

Vampire note:
- Vampire and Necrarch can be accepted into the same blood house.
- Acceptance does not perform Blood Kiss and does not change race.

Dawi note:
- Female Dawi Hero/assets remain disabled.
- Accepted Dawi successor must be male.
- Abstract Dawi marriage/heir system remains active.

Removed:
- Standalone Dynastic Marriage remains removed.
